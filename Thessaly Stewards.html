A mind that stays with your family for forty years.

A Thessaly steward lives in your home, learns your household slowly, and never forgets what you asked it to keep. You own its memory. We only tend the mind.

How to leave us

You should read this before you sign anything.

2040 note · exit firstExit terms sit on the front page, beside the pitch. A company that hides the door does not get a second look.

  • Take everything

    Export the full memory in Open Memory Format 2. The median export takes six minutes, and any licensed steward can read it.

  • Weights in escrow

    If Thessaly fails or is sold, the model weights release to the members' co-operative. Your steward keeps running.

  • Forgetting is real

    Ask it to forget and it does. The ledger records that a deletion happened and never what was deleted.
